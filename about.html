About Moonlit Manhattan

The Business

Moonlit Manhattan is a real clothing brand being developed by Ramis Akram. Its style combines gothic influences with a simple, elegant appearance.

The collection includes a leather jacket, a long-sleeve shirt, a hoodie, and a henley. Black is the main color, with subtle details and occasional dark red accents.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create distinctive clothing that combines dark style, understated elegance, and thoughtful details.

Who We Design For

The brand is intended for young adults who enjoy streetwear and want clothing with personality and restrained branding.
