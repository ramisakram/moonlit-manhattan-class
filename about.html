About Moonlit Manhattan

The Business

My name is Ramis, and Moonlit Manhattan is a clothing brand I am working on. I wanted to create something that matches my interest in darker clothing and gothic details, while still keeping it simple.

The designs I am working on include a leather jacket, a long-sleeve shirt, a hoodie, and a henley. I mainly use black with small details and a few dark red accents.

Our Mission

My goal is to make clothing that stands out through the fit and details without needing a large logo. I want the pieces to feel different but still be easy to wear.

Who We Design For

I am designing for young adults who like streetwear, darker colors, and clothing with a bit of personality.
